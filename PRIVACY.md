# PriceTrail privacy policy

_Last updated: 8 October 2026_

PriceTrail is a browser extension that shows the price history of products on Indian shopping sites and compares prices between stores.

## What PriceTrail stores

When you open a product page on a supported store (Amazon.in, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital, Tata CLiQ, Myntra, AJIO), PriceTrail saves the product's title, image link, page link and price, plus the time it saw that price. It also saves your settings, the products you choose to track and your target prices.

All of this is stored **only in your browser** (`chrome.storage.local`). PriceTrail has no server, no account, no analytics and no advertising. The developer never receives your data.

## Which websites PriceTrail contacts

- **The supported stores** — to read prices on the page you are viewing, to search other stores for the same product, and to re-check the price of products you track. These requests are made from your browser like a normal visit, so they include your usual cookies for that store.
- **archive.org (Wayback Machine)** — the first time you open a product, to read older archived copies of that product page. Only the product's page address is sent. This can be turned off in Settings.
- **Keepa (graph.keepa.com)** — only if you click "Show Amazon's 1-year chart"; the Amazon product ID is sent to load the chart image.
- **GitHub (api.github.com)** — if you installed PriceTrail from GitHub, once a day to see whether a newer version exists. No personal data is sent.

## Your control

You can export, import or delete all PriceTrail data from the popup → Settings → Your data. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.
